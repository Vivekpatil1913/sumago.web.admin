# 05 — User Journeys

## Audience priority
Design first for **enterprise decision-makers**. Everything else is secondary.

### Primary personas
1. **The CEO / Founder / MD** — cares about business outcomes, risk, trust, partnership longevity. Skims; needs proof fast. Wins on: case studies with ROI, founder credibility, transparent process.
2. **The CTO / CIO / Product Head** — cares about engineering quality, architecture, scalability, security, delivery process. Wins on: capabilities depth, tech rigor, the 9-step approach, real team expertise.
3. **The Operations / Transformation Head** — cares about efficiency, automation, measurable improvement. Wins on: industry-specific solutions, workflow/automation outcomes.

### Secondary personas
HR/recruiters (Careers), developers (Careers, Innovation), students, partners, investors, media.

## The core emotional journey
`Curiosity → Interest → Business Understanding → Trust → Confidence → Conversation → Partnership`

Each scroll/section should advance the visitor one step and reduce uncertainty.

| Stage | Visitor state | What the site must do | Where it happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curiosity | "What is this?" | Striking, premium hero; signal seriousness | Hero, 3D logo reveal |
| Interest | "Tell me more" | Founder speaks; clear positioning | Founder welcome, positioning |
| Understanding | "Do they get my problem?" | Frame challenges in business terms | Challenges We Solve, Capabilities, Industries |
| Trust | "Can I believe them?" | Real proof — stories, people, process, metrics | Success Stories, Trust Indicators, Why Sumago |
| Confidence | "They can do this" | Depth, innovation, transparency | Innovation, process, leadership |
| Conversation | "Let's talk" | Frictionless, inviting CTA | Start Your Journey |
| Partnership | "Long-term" | Signal post-delivery commitment | Commitment messaging, support model |

## Key journey flows
- **Executive fast-path:** Home hero → Proof of Impact → Why Sumago → Start Your Journey. (Must be reachable in ≤2 clicks.)
- **Technical evaluator:** Home → Capabilities → individual capability → Innovation → Careers/team. Needs depth and rigor.
- **Recruit:** Home/Careers → culture, team, Founder's Desk → open roles.
- **Returning visitor:** Founder's Desk video series, Innovation/blog updates.

## Friction principles
- Never ask for more than necessary at conversion; "Start Your Journey" should feel like a conversation, not a form gauntlet.
- Every page ends with an obvious, low-pressure next step.
- Reduce uncertainty before asking for commitment — proof precedes the ask.
