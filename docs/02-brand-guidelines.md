# 02 — Brand Guidelines

## Brand essence
Sumago is a **trusted digital transformation partner** — confident, calm, transparent, human. Enterprise-grade but never sterile. The brand sells *confidence*, not features.

- **Tagline:** *"Strive With Technology…!"* (use as the brand's signature line; treat capitalization/styling as in the official logo).
- **Brand acronym — SUMAGO:** **S**olutions · **U**nity · **M**odernization · **A**gility · **G**rowth · **O**wnership. A ready-made storytelling device — consider an animated reveal of the acronym as a brand moment.
- **Trust marks to feature prominently:** **ISO 9001:2015** and **CMMI Maturity Level 5** badges (top-tier credibility — see [05](05-user-journeys.md), [08](08-content-strategy.md)).

## Voice & tone
- **Confident, not boastful.** State capability plainly; let outcomes do the bragging.
- **Calm and clear.** Short sentences. No jargon walls. No hype.
- **Human.** Founder-led, people-forward. We talk *with* the visitor, not *at* them.
- **Outcome-first.** Lead with the client's problem and the business result.

### Words we use / avoid
| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use |
|---|---|
| "We provide / offer / are…" | "Helping businesses…", "Solving…", "Transforming…" |
| "Cutting-edge solutions" (empty) | Specific outcomes + numbers |
| "Best-in-class", "synergy" | Plain, concrete language |
| Feature lists | Business impact, then the technology behind it |

## Color (restrained, premium)
- **Primary:** Brand Red (from logo)
- **Neutrals:** White, Black, Dark Gray, Light Gray
- **Accent (sparingly):** Technology Blue
Use color with discipline: mostly neutral canvas, red for brand moments and key CTAs, blue only as a sparing tech accent. Generous whitespace is part of the palette.

## Typography (principle level — values in [03](03-design-system.md))
Large confident headings · short paragraphs · strong hierarchy · high readability. Typography communicates professionalism.

## Imagery — authentic only (in production)
**No stock photography in the live site, ever.** Use only real: office, employees, meetings, workshops, events, products, clients. Where a real asset doesn't exist yet at launch, use a clearly marked `[REAL ASSET NEEDED]` slot — never a stock photo in production.
**Preview exception:** during the build, preview environments may use flagged stock media + sample copy so layouts read realistically — all swapped for real assets before launch and gated out of production. See [17 — Preview Content Policy](17-placeholder-and-seed-content.md).

## Video
Founder welcome · client stories · office walkthrough · innovation lab · development process · behind-the-scenes · employee stories. **Conversational, not promotional.**

## Iconography & illustration
- Prefer a single consistent icon set (line style, even weight).
- **No generic stock illustrations.** If illustration is needed, use custom, brand-aligned work.

## Don'ts (brand-breaking)
Generic templates · overused card grids · cheap gradients · stock photos/illustrations · decorative-only animation · cluttered layouts without whitespace.
