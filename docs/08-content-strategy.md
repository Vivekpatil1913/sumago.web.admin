# 08 — Content Strategy

## Voice rules (apply to all copy)
- **Outcome-first.** Name the business problem, then the solution, then the technology.
- **Banned openers:** "We provide / We offer / We are / We develop software."
- **Preferred verbs:** Helping · Transforming · Solving · Accelerating · Building · Modernizing · Unlocking.
- Short paragraphs. Strong headings. **Specific numbers beat adjectives.**
- Every section should reduce the visitor's uncertainty about trusting Sumago.
- Write for an executive skim first, depth on demand.

## Trust content (woven throughout)
Founder videos · leadership/expert profiles · real office & team photos · real client stories · the transparent **9-step delivery process** · security practices · support model · case studies · business metrics · certifications · awards. **Demonstrate trust, don't claim it.**

## Content governance
- All content in **Sanity** (see [10](10-technical-architecture.md)); no hardcoded marketing copy.
- Each Success Story and Capability is a structured document, not free-form HTML.
- Maintain a content review step for voice compliance before publish.
- Never publish `[VERIFY]` facts; never use stock imagery.
- During development, unfinished content uses **branded placeholders + clearly-flagged dummy seed data** ([17](17-placeholder-and-seed-content.md)) — never stock photos, and a build gate blocks any placeholder content from reaching production.
