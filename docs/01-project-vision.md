# 01 — Project Vision

## The objective
This is not a redesign. It is a reimagining of Sumago Infotech's complete digital presence into a **world-class Digital Headquarters** that establishes trust, showcases expertise, tells compelling stories, and converts visitors into long-term clients.

The site is simultaneously a: Digital Headquarters · Business Development Platform · Trust-Building Platform · Knowledge Hub · Employer Branding Platform · Innovation Showcase · Digital Experience Platform.

## The one question
Every screen, sentence, and animation must move the visitor toward answering:

> **"Why should I trust Sumago with my business?"**

Everything else supports this. **Sell confidence, not services.**

## Positioning shift
| From | To |
|---|---|
| Software development company | Digital Transformation Partner |
| IT service provider | Enterprise Product Engineering Company |
| Web & mobile development shop | AI & Innovation Company / Strategic Technology Partner |

Message moves from *"We develop software"* → *"We solve business problems through technology."*

## Website philosophy
The site should not behave like a brochure. It should feel like **entering Sumago's headquarters** — visitors *experience* the company rather than read about it. They should feel: *these people understand my business · they've solved problems like mine · they know what they're doing · they're transparent · I can trust them · they'll stay after delivery.*

## The emotional journey
`Curiosity → Interest → Business Understanding → Trust → Confidence → Conversation → Partnership`

The site gradually reduces uncertainty and builds credibility at each scroll.

## What "great" looks like
The site must not be remembered as "a good IT company website." It must feel like walking into Sumago's digital headquarters — combining business strategy, storytelling, human connection, premium design, purposeful motion, and modern engineering, so visitors *already know, trust, and want to work with Sumago before the first meeting.*

## Proof points to surface (real — see [COMPANY-PROFILE](../COMPANY-PROFILE.md))
Since 2013 (13+ years) · 700+ projects · 70+ team · 50+ government / 500+ domestic / 60+ international clients · **ISO 9001:2015 + CMMI Level 5** · 3 offices (Nashik Govind Nagar HQ + Satpur, Pune) · marquee clients (Mahindra, Toyota, Hinduja, MSBTE, FSSAI, NIC, Govt of Maharashtra). These are the credibility backbone — weave them through the trust narrative, don't dump them in one list.

## Success metrics (qualitative + quantitative)
- **Trust signals engaged:** founder video plays, case-study reads, leadership profile views.
- **Conversion:** "Start Your Journey" submissions from enterprise domains.
- **Quality bar:** Lighthouse Perf ≥95, A11y/SEO/Best-Practices 100 (see [14 — Testing](14-testing.md)).
- **Engagement depth:** scroll completion on homepage; time on Success Stories.

## Future vision
Architected to evolve into a **Digital Experience Platform**: client portal, AI assistant, business assessments, proposal generator, support center, knowledge base, partner portal. Build modular and scalable now.
